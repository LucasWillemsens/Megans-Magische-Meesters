Clicking the face-up clone inside a flip-hologram re-fires the no-op else-branch of `onFaceDownCardClick`, stages another flip cookie, and consumes one of the player's remaining flip actions even though the card is already flipped.

## Behavior

- First click on a face-down lane card flips it and increments `staged.flips` exactly once.
- Subsequent clicks on the resulting face-up flip-hologram do not increment `staged.flips` again and do not stage a duplicate `flipFaceUp=true` cookie.
- The play-hologram flip flow (click a play hologram to flip a played card face-up) still works exactly once; further clicks on the now-face-up play hologram are no-ops.
- The face-down click handler still respects the existing `flips <= 0` budget guard.

## Code locations

- `var/www/static/cardDragDrop.js` — `onFaceDownCardClick`, the if-branch that creates the flipHologram and the one-shot click listener on its face-up clone.

## Implementation notes

- The bug: `card.dataset.stagedFlipped` is only set on the hand ghost (else-branch) and on the play-hologram face-up clone, never on the source lane card. The existing line 1448 guard `if (card.dataset.stagedFlipped === 'true') return;` therefore misses the second click on a flipHologram's face-up clone.
- Minimum fix: set `card.dataset.stagedFlipped = 'true'` on the source lane card right after `card.classList.add('ghost');` in the if-branch. The existing line 1448 guard then catches the second click.
- Belt-and-braces: optionally tighten the one-shot click handler with an explicit check on `sourceCard.dataset.stagedFlipped` to bail out before the listener body runs. Either fix alone resolves the bug; landing both is fine.

## Acceptance criteria

- First flip increments `staged.flips` once.
- Second click on the flipHologram's face-up clone does not increment `staged.flips` and writes no new `flipFaceUp` cookie.
- The play-hologram flip path is unchanged.
- Keyboard-triggered flip (`flipKeyboardFocusedCard`) is also regression-tested for the same behavior.

## Dependencies

- None. One-line JS fix; no template, CSS, model or migration changes.
