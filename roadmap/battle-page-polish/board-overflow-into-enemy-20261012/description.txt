Player lanes cannot overflow into the enemy board: overflow cards are clipped because `.playerScreen` and `ul.lanes` are hard-capped at fixed heights and the player board can never grow vertically.

## Behavior

- The player board grows when its lanes grow, pushing the enemy boards up (the enemy board may scroll off-screen on long games).
- The deckHand (deck stack + hand fan + end-turn button) stays at the viewport bottom, fixed size.
- The lane indicator `<p>` and the trustedCards row stay anchored inside the lane regardless of overflow rows.
- Mobile (`@media (max-width: 700px)`) is untouched.

## Code locations

- `var/www/static/cards.css` — `.playerScreen`, `.playerBoard`, `li.playerBoard > ul.basic-mat`, `ul.lanes`, `ul.lanes li.lane > p` rules.

## Implementation notes

- Drop the fixed `height: 50%` from `li.playerBoard > ul.basic-mat` and `height: 85%` from `ul.lanes`; let them grow with their content via flex column / stretch.
- Make `li.playerBoard > ul.basic-mat` a flex column so the player board can expand vertically; keep the deckHand pinned to the bottom with `margin-top: auto`.
- Leave the overflow rows' absolute positioning alone — once the lane grows, the rows simply have room.
- Tighten `test_lane_heights_stretch_equal_across_board` to assert `ul.lanes` itself has no fixed `height:` and add a test that the player board grows for long lanes while the deckHand height stays unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- A player lane with 16 cards renders 2 visible rows; with 32 cards, 3 visible rows.
- The four lanes stay equal-height when one lane is much taller than the others.
- The deckHand height is identical at empty state vs 16 cards in lanes + 12 cards in hand.
- The enemy board is pushed up by the player board growing.
- The lane indicator and trustedCards row stay anchored inside the lane.
