Rendered-DOM tests pin the dead attributes as if they were the live contract.
  - The backtick overlay paragraph shows deck/hand counts plus a per-turn d/p/f counter span that no JS ever updates — the numbers are render-time only and go stale the moment a play or flip is staged.
- Budget-coupling risk (read before implementing subgoal A step 3): replacing the client formula with pure subtraction of staged counts from the shipped budgets is NOT behavior-preserving. In the server formula, staged plays/flips shrink the draw budget only past a headroom (the speed-derived allowance minus this turn's committed flips+plays), and staged plays shrink the flip budget only past their own headroom; below those thresholds staging does not touch those budgets at all. The play budget, by contrast, is a plain clamped subtraction of staged plays+flips. The server therefore ships whatever derived values make the client update a pure clamped-delta application (for example the three computed budgets plus the two per-budget headrooms, or an equivalent set), so the invariant holds: mid-turn remaining budgets equal what the server enforces if the staged cookies were posted now. Do not ship the raw stats and re-derive.
