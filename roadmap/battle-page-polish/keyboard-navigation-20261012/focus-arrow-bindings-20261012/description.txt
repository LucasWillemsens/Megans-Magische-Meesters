Wire `_addHoverArrow` to fire on `focusin` for: (a) a played hand card, (b) a selected ghost of a played card, (c) a face-down lane card that has been flipped. The new `KeyboardSelectionControl` emits `focusin`/`focusout` events on the right elements; this goal fills in the `_addHoverArrow` calls at those hook points.

## Behavior

- Focusing a played hand card (with a staged play hologram in the lane) shows the curved arrow from the hand card to the hologram.
- Focusing a hand ghost shows the curved arrow from the ghost to the staged play hologram.
- Focusing a flipped face-down lane card shows the curved arrow from the lane card to the flip hologram in the holoRow.
- Blurring the focused element removes the hover arrow.
- The existing mouseenter/mouseleave behavior of `_addHoverArrow` is unchanged.
- Confirming a play does not double-create the arrow; the focus-driven and stage-driven arrows are visually one.

## Code locations

- `var/www/static/cardDragDrop.js` — `KeyboardSelectionControl.onFocusIn` / `onFocusOut` stubs (filled in here); focusin/focusout delegation on `playerScreen` (capture phase); `clearKeyboardSelection` cleanup.

## Implementation notes

- `_addHoverArrow` already binds focusin/focusout on its source/target; the new control routes through it on the right hook points.
- `_focusArrowTarget(card)` resolves the target from the card's role: played hand card → keyboard-staged hologram in the lane; hand ghost → staged play hologram (lookup via `hologram.dataset.stagedForCardId === card.dataset.cardId`); flipped face-down lane card → flip hologram in the holoRow.
- Delegation: register `focusin`/`focusout` listeners on `playerScreen` in capture phase (focus events do not bubble) and dispatch to the control's `onFocusIn` / `onFocusOut`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The three focus-driven arrow cases (played hand card, ghost, flipped face-down card) render the arrow to the correct target.
- Blur removes the arrow.
- Mouseenter/mouseleave behavior unchanged.
- No double arrow when the focus-driven and stage-driven arrows overlap visually.

## Notes

- The focus-driven path uses a small `_createFocusArrow(sourceEl, targetEl)` helper that wraps `_createCurvedArrow` without re-binding focus listeners — avoids double-binds.
- The control's `onFocusOut` is a no-op; the existing `_addHoverArrow` focusout listener already debounces removal by 100ms.
- Focus delegation selector covers hand `.cardContainer`, lane `.cardContainer`, and hologram `.cardContainer`. Trusted-cards row is not in the focus delegation.
