Remove the old "handcard action" (the per-card play form whose submit button plays a hand
card to its own type lane on click) and merge hand-card interaction with number-key
selection: selecting a card (via number keys or by clicking it) focuses it, and the orange
selection styling is unified with regular focus styling.

Subgoals (land in this order):
1. `hand-markup-form-removal/` — strip `cardActionForm`/`cardActionButton` from own-hand
   fan cards; keep bare hidden `card_id` AND `lane` inputs (both are JS contracts);
   add `tabindex="-1"` to playable cards; update the three affected tests.
2. `selection-focus-click-to-select/` — focus the card on keyboard selection, blur on
   clear; click-to-select with correct ordinal + digit buffer, drag-safe.
3. `unified-selection-styling/` — replace the orange `.keyboard-selected` treatment with
   styling matching regular focus; align preview hologram accent.
