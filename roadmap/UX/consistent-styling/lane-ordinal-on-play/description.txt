When a card is played to a lane, its laneOrdinal should become the highest laneOrdinal in that lane.

## Desired behavior

- Playing a card to a lane must place it **on top** of the stacked cards already in that lane: its `laneOrdinal` = `max(laneOrdinal of cards in that lane) + 1` (or `1` when the lane is empty).
- This applies to both player plays and bot plays, and to special-action plays (intSpecial plays random hand cards to lanes, spdSpecial rushes cards, etc.).
- The lane stacking visuals (`.cardRow` with ordinal-based z-index) rely on correct ordinals: a played card that keeps an old/duplicate ordinal renders underneath or at a wrong offset.

## Current state (verify first)

`BattleParticipant.playCard()` in `MMM/models.py` already does:
```python
gameCard.state.updateOrdinal(self.getNewMaxCardInLaneOrdinal(lane))
```
and `getNewMaxCardInLaneOrdinal()` returns `max(...) + 1` (or 1 when empty). This appears to satisfy the requirement for the normal play path.

**Verify** whether any path still sets a stale/incorrect laneOrdinal on play:
- Special actions in `MMM/views.py` that call `playCard()` with `specialClause=True` — do they go through the same `updateOrdinal` call? (playCard does it unconditionally after `state.play(lane)`, so it should be fine — confirm.)
- Any direct `GameCard.state.laneOrdinal` assignment or `state.play()` calls outside `playCard()` (e.g. `drawCard()`, starting-card init, shuffle) that could leave a card with a wrong ordinal when it lands in a lane.
- `getNewMaxCardInLaneOrdinal` counts only non-trusted cards in `state__lane=lane` — check whether trusted cards in the same lane should count toward the max (they render in a separate `.cardRow`, so probably not — document the decision).
