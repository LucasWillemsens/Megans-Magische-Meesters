Fix hand card vertical spacing and readability on the battle board.

## Issues to fix

### 1. Margin-top for hand cards

The hand cards (`ul.hand li.cardContainer`) have a broken vertical offset:
- `.playingCards ul.hand` is `height: 8em` with `margin-bottom: 3.5em`
- `.playingCards ul.hand li` is `position: absolute; bottom: 0` and each `li.cardContainer` has `width: calc(4.75rem + 6px); height: calc(6.5rem + 6px)`

The result: hand cards sit too low / overlap the lanes, or have inconsistent top spacing. Hand cards should have a fixed, correct margin-top so the whole row aligns consistently.

### 2. Every hand card's cardType is unreadable

In `cards.css` the `.smallCardInfo .cardType` rule uses:
```css
.smallCardInfo .cardType{
    margin-block-start: 0px;
    margin-bottom: -2.75rem;   /* negative margin pushes it out of place */
    font-size: 7px;
    ...
}
```

The negative `margin-bottom: -2.75rem` combined with the tiny `7px` font makes the type label invisible or overlapping inside hand cards. The cardType must be readable on every hand card.

### 3. Face cards are really low

Static face cards (rank-j/q/k, rendered via `.playingCards .card.rank-j` etc.) sit too low in the card. The CSS-playing-cards framework positions the big suit symbol (`rank-j .suit:after` etc.) at `bottom: .25em` with `font-size: 3em`, which collides with the `.smallCardInfo` footer and makes the face look sunken.

### 4. Other static cards are also too low

Non-face static cards (`rank-2` through `rank-10`, `rank-a`) have their center suit symbols (`suit:after` with `\A`-separated suit stacks) rendered low in the card, near the footer. The static card body should be vertically centered within the card, with the `.smallCardInfo` footer at the very bottom.

## Approach

- Fix `.smallCardInfo .cardType` spacing: remove the negative bottom margin, use a sensible margin and font-size (e.g. 0.6–0.75rem, readable), and ensure it is contained within `.smallCardInfo`.
- Ensure `ul.hand li.cardContainer` has a consistent `margin-top` (the `.hand` container and `.deckHand` flex row should align card tops or bottoms consistently — pick what the board layout intends and make it uniform).
- For static cards in the hand, constrain the suit symbols so they don't collide with the footer: e.g. reduce `font-size` of the `.suit:after` symbol stack inside `.smallCard` or add `padding-bottom` to the card body.
- Face cards: adjust the bottom offset of the `rank-j/q/k .suit:after` big symbol so it sits centered in the visible area above the footer.
- Keep the changes scoped to hand cards and static cards; do not regress the non-static cards with images.

## Files to modify

- `var/www/static/cards.css` — `.smallCardInfo .cardType`, `.playingCards ul.hand` spacing, static card symbol positioning

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hand cards have a fixed, correct margin-top (no overlap with lanes, consistent row)
- [ ] The cardType label is readable on every hand card (font-size ≥ 0.6rem, no negative-margin clipping)
- [ ] Static face cards sit at a normal height in the card, not sunken
- [ ] Static non-face cards' suit symbols are centered, not pushed into the footer
- [ ] Non-static (image) cards are unaffected
