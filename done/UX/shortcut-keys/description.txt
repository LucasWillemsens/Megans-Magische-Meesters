Add accessible keyboard controls for selecting cards, drawing, and ending a turn while reusing the existing staged-action system.

Implementation:
1. Keep all board shortcuts behind shared guards: ignore `input`, `textarea`, `select`, and content-editable targets; ignore enemy-phase boards (`#turnPhase[data-phase="enemy"]`); ignore loading/timeline transitions; and honor `.blocked`, disabled controls, and `CardDragDropSystem.remainingAllowances()`.
2. Make `keyboard-card-selection/` establish the keyboard-selection state and reusable key filtering/cleanup behavior. The selection must be a preview only until confirmation; it must not write a cookie prematurely.
3. Make `confirm-and-navigate/` consume that state: reuse `createupdateCookie()` with the exact drag/drop payload, move the preview between the four drop zones, and cleanly cancel or confirm it.
4. Add `hold-to-end-turn/` and then `hold-to-draw/`. They share a one-second hold timer, early-release cleanup, repeat-key protection, visible loading feedback, and a window/visibility cancellation path. Keep their form submissions identical to clicking the existing controls.
5. Integrate after `large-hand-rendering/` so a numeric card index is stable across all 13-card fan elements. Verify the whole flow with browser automation or a documented manual keyboard matrix in addition to `python3 manage.py test`.

Subgoals and safe order:
1. `keyboard-card-selection/` — direct 1–9 selection, no-zero multi-digit parsing, eligible-card filtering, face-up hologram preview, and no-cookie selection state.
2. `confirm-and-navigate/` — Space/Enter confirmation, arrow-key lane navigation, drop-zone confirmation, Escape/0 cancellation, and ghost/allowance parity with drag/drop.
3. `hold-to-end-turn/` — hold **e** for one second, show progress, submit once, and cancel on early release.
4. `hold-to-draw/` — hold **d** for one second, show progress on the drawable deck, submit once, and cancel on early release.

The two hold leaves are conceptually independent of card selection, but the recommended order serializes them because all four leaves touch `var/www/static/cardDragDrop.js` and the end-turn leaf can define the shared hold-indicator CSS. If parallel work is unavoidable, isolate the shared helper/CSS changes before merging.

- `var/www/static/cardDragDrop.js` — selection state, keyboard event guards, hologram preview/confirmation, lane navigation, and hold timers; do not add a second cookie writer.
