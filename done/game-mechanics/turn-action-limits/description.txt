Per-turn counters on BattleParticipant gate actions: draws limited to intCount+1 (shrinks when over-playing speed), face-up flips to spdCount+1 (shrinks when over-playing intelligence), plays to 2+tactics minus cards already drawn/flipped. Exceeding a limit raises an exception that surfaces as an on-board error message. resetTurn() zeroes the counters on end turn.
