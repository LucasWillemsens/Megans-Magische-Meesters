specialActions(participant) fires when a participant draws their last deck card; the dominant lane stat wins (ties checked int → spd → vis → res). intSpecial plays random hand cards face-up ("master plan"); spdSpecial rushes a lower-power opponent (firing the opponent's specials) or flees from a slower opponent; visSpecial attacks — without a trustable resolve card the attacker is defeated, otherwise a lower-resolve opponent is defeated and a resolve card is trusted; resSpecial trusts up to resCount random revealed cards. Flee/defeat set flags on BattleParticipant.
