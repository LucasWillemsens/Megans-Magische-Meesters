## Description
Everything on a page serves the purpose of the page: whatever is in view works. On every page of Megans Magische Meesters, an interactive control that cannot be used makes clear and logical why it cannot — for example, the player has used up their moves for that turn. Such controls are unmistakably blocked: greyed out, unresponsive, and on hover showing the disabled/forbidden pointer symbol together with the explanation in the title attribute of the element. A state that disables actions blocks every affected control this way; nothing appears clickable that does not work.
